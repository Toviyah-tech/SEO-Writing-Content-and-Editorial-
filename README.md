### On-Page SEO Toolkit & Execution
Every article is delivered with a complete on-page optimization package designed for search visibility and high click-through rates:
- **Focus Keyphrase & Secondary Keywords:** Search-intent-aligned primary phrases supported by semantic subtopics to capture broader organic traffic.
- **Meta Title & Meta Description:** Click-optimized search snippets crafted within character limits to maximize SERP click-through rates (CTR).
- **Featured Image Alt Text & Captions:** Accessibility-compliant alt attributes for Google Image indexing paired with context-rich editorial captions.
- **Content Tags:** Structured taxonomy tagging to strengthen internal linking and topical clustering across the publication.
